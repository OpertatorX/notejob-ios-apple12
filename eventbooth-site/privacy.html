Event Booth Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Event Booth is designed to work locally on your device. Event photos, videos, voice messages, guestbook entries and signatures are stored on the device by default.

Event Booth does not use third-party advertising, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not require an account. Event media leaves the device only when you intentionally use an export or share feature, or when an operating-system service you choose handles that share.

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. OperatorX does not receive your full payment-card details.

You are responsible for informing guests and obtaining any permissions required by applicable law before recording or sharing their media.

For privacy questions: contact.operatorx@proton.me
