OX Invoice is a focused quote and invoice maker for freelancers, independent contractors, and small businesses.

Create a professional estimate, turn it into an invoice when the job is approved, export a polished PDF, and keep track of what is sent, accepted, paid, or overdue - without a complicated accounting suite.

FAST DOCUMENTS
- Create estimates and invoices in a few steps
- Save clients automatically for faster reuse
- Reuse line items, quantities, rates, discounts, taxes, deposits, and due dates
- Convert an estimate into an invoice
- Duplicate an existing document when you need a similar one

PROFESSIONAL PDFs
- Preview the document before sharing
- Choose from Classic, Modern, and Minimal layouts
- Add your business details and logo
- Export and share a clean A4 PDF
- Support for EUR, USD, GBP, and CHF

SIMPLE TRACKING
- See unpaid, paid, overdue, and pending totals at a glance
- Search and filter estimates and invoices
- Mark estimates as accepted and invoices as paid
- Keep client and document history on your device

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your business, client, and document content is stored locally on your device. OX Invoice has no advertising and no tracking SDK.

OX Invoice includes three free documents so you can test the complete workflow before choosing OX Invoice Pro. Pro is offered as an auto-renewable subscription. Prices and availability shown by the App Store apply.
