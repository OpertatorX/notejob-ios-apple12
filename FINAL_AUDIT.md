## Corrections applied in the final audit

1. **US first-launch localization** — a non-French device now starts in English + USD instead of French + EUR.
2. **iOS base localization** — `CFBundleDevelopmentRegion` is now English, matching the primary App Store locale.
3. **Storage write ordering** — app-data writes are serialized to prevent an older async save from overwriting a newer state.
4. **Paywall price integrity** — fake hard-coded Store prices were removed. Purchase buttons stay disabled until StoreKit returns the localized price.
5. **Paywall localization integrity** — removed the hard-coded `€/$ per month` equivalent that could be wrong outside a specific storefront.
6. **Date validation** — invoice due date / estimate validity date cannot be before issue date.
7. **Data export** — local backup is exported as a real JSON file, not a potentially huge share-message string.
8. **Accessibility baseline** — shared buttons and text fields expose button/input labels and disabled state to accessibility APIs; bottom navigation exposes selection state.
9. **France scope disclosure** — French store/legal/review copy now states that a PDF generated by OX Invoice is not a replacement for a regulated electronic-invoicing platform where French law requires one.
10. **Brand consistency** — web mark uses OX branding instead of the old QuoteFlow mark.

## Revenue strategy for launch

The product is positioned as a low-complexity alternative to invoice apps with materially higher recurring prices. Launch pricing is intentionally aggressive:

- 3 full documents free: user sees PDF quality before the paywall.
- €9.99 / 6 months.
- €16.99 / year as the value anchor.
- No ads, no monthly plan at launch.

Do **not** reduce the price further at launch. The first monetization objective is to measure install → first document → third document → paywall → purchase. Once enough real conversions exist, test a higher annual price before adding features or discounts.

## Subscription review risk

An auto-renewable subscription must deliver ongoing value. OX Invoice currently provides ongoing unlimited creation while subscribed, purchase restoration across the user's Apple devices, and commits to ongoing maintenance/feature updates. Keep shipping substantive improvements. If App Review objects specifically to recurring billing for this local-first utility, the safest fallback is a non-consumable/lifetime Pro unlock rather than weakening the product.

## France e-invoicing

OX Invoice is a PDF estimate/invoice creator; it is not a French approved e-invoicing platform. Do not market it as regulatory e-invoicing compliance. The product can still serve estimates, B2C/out-of-scope documents and PDF workflows; regulated B2B electronic invoicing must use the required structured/platform process when applicable.
