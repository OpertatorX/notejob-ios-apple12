Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

OX Invoice is published by OperatorX. This policy explains how OX Invoice handles information in the iOS app.

Local-first document data

Your business profile, client details, estimates, invoices, line items, notes, settings, and generated-document history are stored locally on your device. OX Invoice does not operate an account system and does not upload this document content to an OperatorX server.

Business logo and Photos

Photo Library access is requested only if you choose a business logo. The selected image is stored with your local OX Invoice data and used in document previews and PDF exports. OX Invoice does not upload the selected logo.

PDFs and exports

PDF generation occurs on your device. When you choose Share or Export, iOS presents the system share sheet and you decide where the information is sent. OperatorX does not receive those exports.

Purchases

OX Invoice Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. Purchase proofs are verified using the OpenIAP/IAPKit verification service so the app can determine whether Pro access is active. For this purpose, IAPKit processes and retains transaction and entitlement records, which can include store- or app-scoped transaction identifiers. OX Invoice does not receive your payment-card details.

Advertising and tracking

OX Invoice contains no advertising SDK and does not use advertising tracking. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Analytics

The current version does not include a third-party product analytics SDK.

Retention and deletion

Local OX Invoice content remains on your device until you delete documents, clear/remove the app data, or uninstall the app, subject to your device backups. Purchase records are retained by Apple and the verification provider according to their respective policies.

Children

OX Invoice is a business utility and is not directed to children.
