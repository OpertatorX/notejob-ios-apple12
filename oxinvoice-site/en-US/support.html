Support

Need help with OX Invoice? We want document creation and PDF export to be straightforward.

Email support

contact.operatorx@proton.me

Purchases

Use More → Restore Purchases after reinstalling or changing devices. Subscription management and cancellation are available in your Apple account settings.

PDF troubleshooting

Open a document, choose Preview, then Share PDF. If a destination app is unavailable, save the PDF to Files first and open it from there.

Data

OX Invoice content is stored locally. Use More → Export my data before device changes if you want an additional copy of your local data.
